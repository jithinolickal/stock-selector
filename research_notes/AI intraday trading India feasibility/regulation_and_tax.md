# Regulatory and Tax Framework for a Retail Individual Running API-Based Intraday Algo Trading in India (Upstox) — as of 25 Oct 2026

Scope note: India only. Primary texts of the SEBI 4 Feb 2025 circular and the NSE implementation circular were not readable via fetch (SEBI HTML pages return only the header; NSE PDF not fetched), so most provision-level detail comes from broker/law-firm summaries and Upstox/Zerodha posts. The SEBI 30 Sep 2025 extension circular WAS read in full (PDF). Items marked "unverified" or "unclear" should be checked against the primary PDF before relying on them.

## Key Question 1: SEBI retail algo framework (circular of 4 Feb 2025) — exact requirements and what applies to a self-coded personal algo below the threshold

### Takeaway
SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/0000013 (4 Feb 2025) routes all retail algo/API trading through brokers (broker = principal, algo provider = agent), mandates exchange-issued algo IDs on API orders, static whitelisted IPs, OAuth + 2FA, and splits algos into white-box (execution) vs black-box (provider must be a SEBI-registered Research Analyst). The original 1 Aug 2025 start was moved to 1 Oct 2025 and then put on a glide path; the framework became binding on ALL brokers from 1 Apr 2026. A self-coded personal algo at or below 10 orders/second needs no exchange registration but does need a static IP, a single API key, and is auto-tagged with a generic algo ID by the broker.

### Cited Findings
- Circular title "Safer participation of retail investors in Algorithmic trading", dated 4 Feb 2025, Circular No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/0000013 — [SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/feb-2025/safer-participation-of-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading_91614.html)
- Brokers act as principals and algo providers as their agents; retail investors get access to approved algos only via registered brokers; broker must obtain exchange permission for each algo it offers — [Mondaq](https://mondaq.com:443/india/commoditiesderivativesstock-exchanges/1581380/sebi-circular-safeguarding-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading); [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/sebi-retail-investors-algorithmic-trading/)
- Open APIs prohibited; access only through a client-specific API key plus a static IP whitelisted by the broker; OAuth-based authentication and 2FA mandatory — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/sebi-retail-investors-algorithmic-trading/)
- "All algo orders flowing through APIs will be tagged with a unique identifier provided by the stock exchange"; brokers must "detect and categorize orders above the threshold as algo orders" — [Mondaq](https://mondaq.com:443/india/commoditiesderivativesstock-exchanges/1581380/sebi-circular-safeguarding-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading)
- Self-developed ("tech-savvy") retail algos must be registered with the exchange through the broker only if they exceed "a specified order per second threshold"; the SEBI circular text as summarised does NOT name the number (the 10 OPS figure comes from the exchange implementation standards) — [Mondaq](https://mondaq.com:443/india/commoditiesderivativesstock-exchanges/1581380/sebi-circular-safeguarding-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading); [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/sebi-retail-investors-algorithmic-trading/)
- Such self-developed registered algos "can only be extended to close family members" — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/sebi-extends-deadline-for-retail-algo-trading-framework/)
- White Box (Execution) algos = logic disclosed and replicable; Black Box = logic not disclosed; black-box providers must register as Research Analysts, maintain research reports per algo, and any change in logic must be registered as a new algo — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/sebi-retail-investors-algorithmic-trading/); [Angel One](https://oga-prod.angelone.in/knowledge-center/online-share-trading/sebi-algo-trading-rules)
- Algo providers are not SEBI-regulated but must be empanelled with exchanges; brokers must do due diligence; fee-sharing between broker and provider allowed if disclosed — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/sebi-retail-investors-algorithmic-trading/)
- Exchanges must: set an SOP for algo testing, run surveillance on all algo orders, be able to kill-switch orders from a given algo ID, issue operational modalities/FAQs covering RMS for API orders, empanelment, registration, confidentiality of retail strategies and data flow — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/sebi-retail-investors-algorithmic-trading/)
- Broker is solely responsible for investor grievances related to algo trading and must monitor APIs for prohibited activities — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/sebi-retail-investors-algorithmic-trading/)
- Timeline: original effective date 1 Aug 2025; extended to 1 Oct 2025 by circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/108 dated 29 Jul 2025 — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/sebi-extends-deadline-for-retail-algo-trading-framework/); [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/sebi/sebi-extends-algo-trading-rule-implementation-1st-october-2025.html)
- Circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/132 dated 30 Sep 2025 (read in full): exchanges issued detailed operational modalities on 22 Jul 2025 and clarifications/modifications in the second fortnight of Sep 2025; brokers that were ready went live 1 Oct 2025; glide path for others: Milestone 1 — apply to register the API-based retail algo product and at least one strategy by 31 Oct 2025; Milestone 2 — complete registration of retail algo products and a few strategies by 30 Nov 2025; Milestone 3 — participate in at least one full mock session by 3 Jan 2026; brokers missing milestones barred from onboarding new retail API-algo clients from 5 Jan 2026; "W.e.f. April 01, 2026, algo framework specified in circular dated February 04, 2025 along with implementation standards and detailed operational modalities (issued by exchanges) will be applicable for all stock brokers." — [SEBI PDF](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/sep-2025/1759232056254.pdf); corroborated by [Bajaj Broking](https://www.bajajbroking.in/share-market-news/sebi-extends-algo-trading-implementation-timeline)
- Legal basis: Section 11(1) SEBI Act 1992 read with Reg. 30 of SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations 1992 — [SEBI PDF](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/sep-2025/1759232056254.pdf)

### Inferences
- For a personal, self-coded algo at <=10 OPS the practical SEBI-level obligations are: use a broker API key (not any "open" API), static whitelisted IP, OAuth/2FA login, accept that the broker tags orders with a generic algo ID, and stay under 10 OPS. No exchange registration, no RA registration, no auditor certificate.
- Because the broker is the principal and is "fully liable", brokers will enforce these rules systemically (reject orders from non-whitelisted IPs, throttle >10 OPS) rather than leave compliance to the user.
- "Close family members" sharing is only relevant if you let relatives run your algo; sharing with anyone else makes you an unregistered algo provider.

### Gaps
- Could not read the SEBI 4 Feb 2025 circular body (sebi.gov.in HTML returns only the header). Exact paragraph numbers, the formal definition of "algo", and the exact wording of the family-member clause are unverified.
- Whether SEBI itself fixes the OPS threshold or delegates it to exchanges is unclear from summaries; all sources attribute "10 OPS" to the NSE standards.

## Key Question 2: NSE/BSE implementation — algo ID registration, API tagging, static IP whitelisting, fees, timelines

### Takeaway
NSE circular NSE/INVG/67858 (published ~5-6 May 2025) sets the operative rules: 10 orders/second per segment per exchange is the registration threshold; below it a generic algo ID is used, above it a strategy must be registered (strategy write-up, RMS write-up, auditor certificate) and gets a unique algo ID; one primary + one backup static IP per API key, changeable once a week; multiple API keys allowed but only one may run an unregistered algo; sessions must end before the next trading day; 5-year audit trail. Detailed operational modalities followed on 22 Jul 2025 with clarifications in Sep 2025.

### Cited Findings
- NSE implementation standards circular file "INVG67858" (NSE/INVG/67858), "Implementation Standards for safer participation of retail investors in Algorithmic trading"; Zerodha's overview dated 6 May 2025 — [Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/general/a-comprehensive-overview-of-nses-circular-on-the-new-retail-algo-trading-framework); [Upstox Community](https://community.upstox.com/t/important-new-sebi-exchange-mandates-for-api-trading-effective-1st-april-2026/14822); [Business Standard, 6 May 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/capital-market-news/nse-lays-down-new-ground-rules-for-retail-algo-trading-to-boost-safety-and-compliance-125050600690_1.html)
- Three algo categories: broker-generated, algo-provider (empanelled third party), client-generated — [Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/general/a-comprehensive-overview-of-nses-circular-on-the-new-retail-algo-trading-framework)
- Individuals may automate up to 10 orders per second per segment per exchange without registration; above 10 OPS exchange registration required; limits apply per API key; only one API key per user may run an unregistered algo — [Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/general/a-comprehensive-overview-of-nses-circular-on-the-new-retail-algo-trading-framework); [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/investing-guide/articles/retail-algo-trading-rules-nse-circular)

- Static IP: one primary and one backup; changes limited to once a week unless justified (technical emergency); IPv6 allowed; IP need not be Indian; 127.0.0.1/private addresses won't work; static IP needed only for order placement (Zerodha reading) — [Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/general/a-comprehensive-overview-of-nses-circular-on-the-new-retail-algo-trading-framework); [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/investing-guide/articles/retail-algo-trading-rules-nse-circular); [Kite Connect forum](https://www.kite.trade/forum/discussion/comment/49638/)

- The circular table said algos must be hosted on broker cloud servers, but Zerodha said that requirement would be revised out — [Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/general/a-comprehensive-overview-of-nses-circular-on-the-new-retail-algo-trading-framework)

- Static IPs may be shared among family members "if compliant with SEBI's December 2024 guidelines" — [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/investing-guide/articles/retail-algo-trading-rules-nse-circular)
- Brokers may charge clients for API access in addition to brokerage; exchanges may deactivate rogue algos — [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/investing-guide/articles/retail-algo-trading-rules-nse-circular)
- Brokers fully liable for all API orders, must run RMS checks and follow IBT/STWT and algo guidelines; exchanges can terminate rogue algos — [Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/general/a-comprehensive-overview-of-nses-circular-on-the-new-retail-algo-trading-framework)
- Exchanges issued detailed operational modalities on 22 Jul 2025 covering empanelment of algo providers, registration of strategies, unique ID assignment; clarifications issued second fortnight Sep 2025 — [SEBI PDF 30 Sep 2025](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/sep-2025/1759232056254.pdf); [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/sebi/sebi-extends-algo-trading-rule-implementation-1st-october-2025.html)
- Zerodha's own 3,000 orders/day cap is internal policy, not the circular; Kite Connect data API cut from Rs 2,000 to Rs 500/month from 6 May 2025; order placement APIs free since Mar 2025; a "Personal" app costs nothing — [Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/general/a-comprehensive-overview-of-nses-circular-on-the-new-retail-algo-trading-framework); [Kite Connect forum](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/comment/49597)

### Inferences
- "Generic algo ID" tagging is done by the broker, not the user; a personal coder's only tagging-related job is to use the broker's API (and any header/param the broker introduces).
- A cloud bot (AWS/GCP etc.) is compatible as long as it has a fixed public IP (elastic IP / NAT) registered as primary; a laptop on home broadband with dynamic IP is not compliant without a static-IP ISP plan or a proxy/VPN with fixed egress IP.
- Weekly-change limit means IP rotation must be designed out of your infra (no autoscaling with ephemeral IPs for the order path).

### Gaps
- No NSE/BSE fee schedule for algo registration/empanelment was found; whether exchanges charge a per-strategy registration fee is unknown.
- NSE circular PDF not fetched; the "10 working days" timeline, Annexure 7 content, and the final status of the auditor-certificate requirement are unverified.
- BSE's parallel implementation circular was not located.

## Key Question 3: How Upstox implemented it — what an API user must do to keep placing orders

### Takeaway
Upstox requires a static IP (primary + optional secondary) whitelisted under "My Apps" for all order-placing endpoints, allows only one active API app per user, auto-tags <=10 OPS orders with generic algo ID 99999, systemically rejects >10 OPS, and from 1 Apr 2026 also disallows plain market orders via API (Market Price Protection is default, with an optional `market_protection` parameter). Read-only/market-data endpoints are not IP-restricted.

### Cited Findings
- Upstox cites SEBI 4 Feb 2025 circular and a Sept 2025 SEBI extension; per Upstox staff, 1 Oct 2025 = brokers/exchanges infra-ready date; 1 Apr 2026 = user adoption hard-stop — [Upstox Community guide](https://community.upstox.com/t/a-guide-for-our-api-algo-partners-and-clients/11826)
- "The Static IP for Algos is a regulatory requirement from SEBI to verify the source of the orders from algos regardless of the Order Per Second (OPS) criteria" — mandatory for direct API users at all volume levels; whitelisting live under "My Apps"; only one app may be active, others go dormant; IP changeable once a week — [Upstox Community guide](https://community.upstox.com/t/a-guide-for-our-api-algo-partners-and-clients/11826)
- Generic algo ID 99999 applied automatically by Upstox for <=10 OPS; >10 OPS requires exchange registration facilitated by Upstox; Upstox monitors OPS and "systemically rejects orders exceeding 10 OPS" — [Upstox Community guide](https://community.upstox.com/t/a-guide-for-our-api-algo-partners-and-clients/11826)
- Upstox classification: any strategy using something other than TWAP or VWAP is deemed black box even if logic is disclosed; black-box provider firm must be SEBI-registered RA; algo ID must be passed with every order or Upstox rejects it (applies to registered algos) — [Upstox Community guide](https://community.upstox.com/t/a-guide-for-our-api-algo-partners-and-clients/11826)
- Algo providers must be empanelled with NSE/BSE (directors need 2+ years securities-market experience) before partnering with Upstox — [Upstox Community guide](https://community.upstox.com/t/a-guide-for-our-api-algo-partners-and-clients/11826)
- Brokerage: all API trades at a flat Rs 10 (Upstox staff reply); Upstox extended "Rs 10 order API pricing till March 2026" — [Upstox Community guide](https://community.upstox.com/t/a-guide-for-our-api-algo-partners-and-clients/11826); [Upstox Community pricing post](https://community.upstox.com/t/stay-ahead-of-the-curve-we-re-extending-10-order-api-pricing-till-march-2026/13277)
- 1 Apr 2026 mandate post (cites NSE/INVG/67858): (1) one active API app per user, most recently created kept, others deleted; (2) each app must have a registered static IP; requests from unregistered/dynamic IPs may be blocked; IP check applies to Place Order, Modify Order, Cancel Order, Place Multi Order, Cancel Multi Order, GTT order; (3) >10 OPS requires exchange registration, <=10 OPS may run unregistered; (4) "MPP to become default, market orders not allowed": Market and SL-M orders placed with market protection, optional `market_protection` parameter, else Upstox applies automatic protection; May 7 2026 update: static IP sharing among family members now supported via "Family Account" — [Upstox Community, Apr 2026 mandates](https://community.upstox.com/t/important-new-sebi-exchange-mandates-for-api-trading-effective-1st-april-2026/14822)
- Earlier (2025) Upstox said that from 1 Oct 2025 all market orders via Upstox would not be processed and that converting market to limit-at-LTP with MPP "adds considerable latency" — [Upstox Community MPP thread](https://community.upstox.com/t/market-price-protection-order-via-api-for-futures/11125)
- Oct 2025 clarification thread (community, partly unofficial): only Order APIs must originate from the registered static IP; IP updatable once a week; the platform refuses to register an IP already registered by another user; a reply claims one IP can be shared with up to 5 immediate family members (unconfirmed by Upstox) — [Upstox Community static IP clarification](https://community.upstox.com/t/clarification-on-static-ip-deadline-for-algo-api-trading-october-1-2025/11613)
- IP whitelisting steps: account.upstox.com/developer/apps → edit app → enter Primary IP — [Upstox Community static IP clarification](https://community.upstox.com/t/clarification-on-static-ip-deadline-for-algo-api-trading-october-1-2025/11613)
- Vendor blog (QuotaGuard, marketing source) says IP updates invalidate the existing access token and that SEBI's static IP mandate is live and enforced by Upstox from 1 Apr 2026; its claim of an "X-Algo-Name" header requirement is NOT confirmed by any Upstox source — [QuotaGuard](https://www.quotaguard.com/blog/configure-upstox-algo-trading-my-apps)
- Upstox footer disclaimer: "We do not authorize any person or organization to conduct trades on behalf of Upstox customers." — [Upstox Community](https://community.upstox.com/t/sebis-algo-trading-rules-regulations/9792)

### Inferences
- Practical checklist for a self-coder on Upstox (post 1 Apr 2026): one API app; register a static public IP (and backup) in My Apps; run the order-placing process only from that IP; keep OAuth daily login (token flow); stay <=10 OPS; expect generic algo ID 99999 tagging automatically; use LIMIT orders or MARKET with `market_protection`; market data/WebSocket and read endpoints can run from anywhere.
- Upstox's "anything non-TWAP/VWAP is black box" classification matters only if you register (>10 OPS) or distribute the algo; an unregistered personal algo isn't classified.

## Key Question 4: SEBI stance on unregulated algo/strategy sellers and "AI trading" subscriptions; legality of auto-executing a bought third-party signal

### Takeaway
SEBI's Sep 2022 circular bars brokers from referencing algo past/expected returns or associating with platforms that do; it followed with show-cause notices to 110+ brokers (2023) and 115+ brokers tied to Tradetron (Oct 2024), and a 2025 settlement scheme. Under the Feb 2025 framework, anyone selling a strategy to others must be an exchange-empanelled algo provider acting as the broker's agent, and a black-box (undisclosed logic) provider must be a SEBI-registered Research Analyst. No SEBI document found explicitly addresses an individual buying an external "AI signal" and auto-executing it through their own API key; this is a grey area.

### Cited Findings
- SEBI circular (Sep 2022; sebi.gov.in URL path "sep-2022", id 62628): brokers providing algo services must not "directly or indirectly make any reference to the past or expected future return/performance of the algorithm", must not associate with any platform doing so, and had 7 days to remove references/disassociate — [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/sebi-circular-on-unregulated-platforms-offering-algorithmic-strategies-claiming-performance-returns/135862); [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/sebi-warns-brokers-to-refrain-from-mis-selling-algo-trading-strategy-or-services-using-inflated-claims/68248.html)
- SEBI viewed high-return marketing and "ratings" of algo strategies on unregulated platforms as mis-selling — [Mondaq](https://mondaq.co.uk/india/securities/1293722/&location=articleauthorphototop)
- 2023: show-cause notices to 110+ brokers (incl. Zerodha, 5Paisa, Motilal Oswal) for links to platforms promising assured returns; Oct 2024: notices to 115+ brokers tied to Tradetron; Mar 2025: SEBI preparing a settlement scheme (Rs 1–2 lakh, 3-month window) — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/sebi-to-launch-settlement-scheme-for-brokers-in-algo-violation-cases); [5paisa](https://5paisa.com/news/sebis-new-algorithmic-trading-regulations-what-brokers-investors-need-to-know)

- SEBI Apr 2025 warning on social-media securities frauds: scammers pose as educators, fake testimonials, promise returns that are "neither feasible nor legal", run fake trading platforms/groups on WhatsApp/Telegram — [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/sebi-warns-investors-stay-away-from-unregistered-platforms-and-apps/76880.html)

- NSE Aug 2026: five caution notices against unregistered persons/channels offering tips, account handling and assured returns; investors told not to hand over trading credentials — [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/nse-warns-investors-against-5-telegram-and-social-media-operators-offering-market-tips-and-account-handling-services/81434.html)

- 2026 enforcement against registered RAs: Streetgains censured (Mar 2026) for explicit/implicit profit assurances; Eqwires censured (Sep 2026) for fixed-return assurances and handling a client trading account — [Moneylife Streetgains](https://www.moneylife.in/article/sebi-censures-streetgains-research-services-bars-it-from-taking-new-clients-for-1-month-over-misleading-return-assurances/80042.html); [Moneylife Eqwires](https://www.moneylife.in/article/sebi-censures-eqwires-research-analyst-for-misleading-claims-handling-client-trading-account/81817.html)
- 2026 SEBI Master Circular for Research Analysts includes provisions on responsible/ethical use of AI in research and recommendations (applies to registered RAs) — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/sebi-master-circular-for-research-analysts-fees-dual-registration-renewals/)

### Inferences
- Writing and running your own model/AI on your own account below 10 OPS is squarely within the "client-generated algo" bucket and is permitted.
- Paying for a third-party AI signal and auto-executing it: if the seller is an exchange-empanelled algo provider plugged in via the broker, it is the intended compliant path. If the seller is an unregistered Telegram/SaaS "AI signals" vendor, the seller is likely operating as an unregistered RA/IA (illegal for them), and the broker's framework offers no legitimate route for their strategy; your own orders would still carry a generic ID as "your" algo, so enforcement risk sits mostly with the seller, but the Eqwires order shows SEBI treats account handling by third parties as a violation. Treat as legal grey zone, not clearly permitted.
- SEBI's repeated theme: any performance/return claim for an algo is a red flag; a product that markets backtested returns to others would itself fall foul of the 2022 circular's spirit.

### Gaps
- No SEBI circular/press release found (2025–2026) specifically addressing "AI trading" subscription services or individuals consuming external signals via API.
- Whether a SEBI-registered RA may itself place orders in a client's account (vs. only recommend) was not confirmed from RA Regulations text.
- Final status of the 2025 broker settlement scheme not confirmed.

## Key Question 5: Taxation of intraday profits (equity and F&O)

### Takeaway
Intraday equity P&L is speculative business income (s.43(5) of the 1961 Act), taxed at slab rates, with losses set off only against speculative gains and carried forward 4 years (return must be filed by the due date); F&O (including intraday F&O) is non-speculative business income (8-year carry-forward, set-off against other business income). ITR-3 is required. Turnover for intraday = sum of absolute profits and absolute losses; tax audit under s.44AB applies above Rs 10 crore turnover when cash receipts/payments are <=5% (Rs 1 crore otherwise), and sources agree s.44AD presumptive scheme excludes speculative business (one source disagrees). From FY 2026-27 the Income-tax Act 2025 applies with renumbered sections (speculative-loss set-off is s.113; s.66 reportedly replaces s.43(5) — unverified).

### Cited Findings
- Intraday equity profits classified as speculative business income under Section 43(5) of the Income Tax Act 1961; taxed at the taxpayer's slab rate — [Sahi](https://www.sahi.com/blogs/income-tax-on-intraday-trading-india); [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/tax-on-speculative-income)
- Speculative losses can be set off only against speculative income; carried forward 4 years; carry-forward requires filing by the due date under s.139(1) — [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/tax-on-speculative-income); [Quicko](https://learn.quicko.com/intraday-equity-trading-tax-treatment)
- F&O (futures and options on recognised exchanges) is non-speculative by proviso (d) to s.43(5); non-speculative losses set off against any other business income (not salary) and carried forward 8 years — [Equentis](https://www.equentis.com/blog/income-tax-on-intraday-trading/); [Groww AY 2026-27 guide](https://groww.in/blog/how-traders-are-taxed-in-india)
- Turnover = |total profits| + |total losses| (absolute sum), brokerage/taxes excluded; Quicko example: Rs 300 profit + Rs 10,000 loss = Rs 10,300 turnover — [Quicko](https://learn.quicko.com/intraday-equity-trading-tax-treatment); [VJM Global](https://www.vjmglobal.com/blog/income-tax-profit-loss-intraday-share-trading)
- s.44AB audit threshold Rs 10 crore when cash transactions <=5% of receipts and payments; "in case of intra-day trading, since no transaction is made in cash, the threshold limit of INR 10 Crores shall apply" — [VJM Global](https://www.vjmglobal.com/blog/income-tax-profit-loss-intraday-share-trading); Groww: audit above Rs 10 crore (digital) / Rs 1 crore (non-digital) — [Groww](https://groww.in/blog/how-traders-are-taxed-in-india)
- Conflicting lower-tier audit view: audit also required if profit <6% of turnover (or loss) and total income exceeds the basic exemption limit (applies via the 44AD opt-out mechanics) — [Quicko](https://learn.quicko.com/intraday-equity-trading-tax-treatment); [Groww](https://groww.in/blog/how-traders-are-taxed-in-india); contradicted by a Quicko-linked Q&A concluding no audit below Rs 10 crore — [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/tax-audit-applicability-on-intraday-trading/182022)
- s.44AD presumptive scheme: "specifically excluded" for speculative businesses — [Equentis](https://www.equentis.com/blog/income-tax-on-intraday-trading/); [CA Sahuja](https://www.casahuja.com/2025/08/guidance-note-correct-business-codes.html); contradicted by Quicko which says intraday traders can opt in — [Quicko](https://learn.quicko.com/intraday-equity-trading-tax-treatment)
- 44AD for F&O (non-speculative): 6% of turnover deemed income if turnover below Rs 3 crore and receipts digital; 5-year lock-in; losses can't be carried under 44AD — [Groww](https://groww.in/blog/how-traders-are-taxed-in-india)
- ITR-3 required for intraday/F&O; ITR-4 only if opting 44AD (non-speculative) and income below Rs 50 lakh — [Groww](https://groww.in/blog/how-traders-are-taxed-in-india); [Quicko](https://learn.quicko.com/intraday-equity-trading-tax-treatment)
- Due dates: 31 Jul (non-audit) / 31 Oct (audit) per Quicko; Groww's AY 2026-27 guide states a 31 Aug 2026 deadline after which carry-forward is lost — [Quicko](https://learn.quicko.com/intraday-equity-trading-tax-treatment); [Groww](https://groww.in/blog/how-traders-are-taxed-in-india)
- Advance tax if liability > Rs 10,000: 15% by 15 Jun, 45% by 15 Sep, 75% by 15 Dec, 100% by 15 Mar; interest under s.234B/234C if missed — [Quicko](https://learn.quicko.com/intraday-equity-trading-tax-treatment); [Groww](https://groww.in/blog/how-traders-are-taxed-in-india)
- Deductible expenses: STT, GST, transaction charges, internet/electricity used for trading; income taxed at slab plus surcharge and 4% cess — [Quicko](https://learn.quicko.com/intraday-equity-trading-tax-treatment)
- Under the new tax regime, brought-forward business losses cannot be set off/carried forward (Quicko's statement; verify) — [Quicko](https://learn.quicko.com/intraday-equity-trading-tax-treatment)
- Income-tax Act 2025: speculative-loss set-off/carry-forward is Section 113 ("set off only against profits and gains of another speculation business"); secondary sources say s.66 replaces s.43(5); Act applies from 1 Apr 2026 / FY 2026-27 (FY 2025-26 still under 1961 Act) — [Income Tax Dept s.113](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-113-98); [Sahi](https://www.sahi.com/blogs/income-tax-on-intraday-trading-india); [AUBSP](https://www.aubsp.com/income-tax-act-2025-section-113)
- STT (per Groww, citing Budget 2026): futures 0.05%, options 0.15% — [Groww](https://groww.in/blog/how-traders-are-taxed-in-india)

### Inferences
- For a Rs 1 lakh account the audit question is academic unless absolute P&L churn exceeds Rs 10 crore; the realistic risk is the "profit <6% / loss + income above exemption" audit trigger, on which sources conflict — get a CA view.
- Because intraday equity losses are ring-fenced, a strategy that loses on cash intraday and gains on F&O cannot net the two for tax; F&O intraday is tax-friendlier (non-speculative).

## Key Question 6: Other practical compliance — peak margin, Upstox auto square-off, margin penalties, SEBI F&O curbs and their effect on a Rs 1 lakh index-options intraday account

### Takeaway
Peak margin (100% since Sep 2021, four random intraday snapshots) with 0.5%/1% shortfall penalties applies; Upstox auto-squares MIS equity and F&O at 3:25 PM (3:10 PM for CAS-eligible stocks) with a Rs 59–88.5 charge. SEBI's 1 Oct 2024 circular (SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD-1/P/CIR/2024/132) raised min index contract value to Rs 15 lakh, left one weekly expiry per exchange (Nifty on NSE—Tuesday since 1 Sep 2025; Sensex on BSE—Thursday), added 2% ELM on expiry-day short options, and mandated upfront option-premium collection (1 Feb 2025) and intraday position-limit monitoring (1 Apr 2025). Current lots: Nifty 65, Bank Nifty 30, Sensex 20. A Rs 1 lakh account is effectively limited to buying a few lots of index options (premium paid upfront), cannot sell naked options, and has only two weekly-expiry days per week to trade.

### Inferences
- Rs 1 lakh account, index options intraday: only long options are feasible (premium must be fully funded upfront since 1 Feb 2025; no intraday leverage on premium). With Nifty lot = 65, each Rs 100 of premium costs Rs 6,500 per lot, so a Rs 150–250 ATM weekly premium implies roughly Rs 10–16k per lot; 2–4 lots is a realistic max with risk buffer. Selling options is not feasible (SPAN+exposure margins per index lot run well above Rs 1 lakh, plus the extra 2% ELM on expiry day) — margin figures are inference, not sourced.
- Only two "expiry-day" opportunities per week (Nifty Tue, Sensex Thu); other indices (Bank Nifty, FinNifty, Midcap) are monthly only, so an intraday 0DTE-style strategy must span both exchanges to trade more than one day a week.
- Auto square-off at 3:25 PM (F&O) means the algo must flatten by ~3:20 PM to avoid the Rs 59–88.5 fee and forced market exits; combined with "no plain market orders via API", exits need limit/MPP orders.
- Peak-margin penalties are mostly a concern for option sellers/futures; a long-options-only intraday bot funded 100% upfront is unlikely to hit a shortfall.
