# Intraday "Spike Fade" / Momentum-Exhaustion Scalping (NSE cash, MIS) — research notes

Scope: shorting the end of a sharp intraday run-up (and mirror-longing sharp drops) in liquid, volatile NSE stocks for a 0.1–0.4% cover. Labels: **COSTED** = net of stated transaction costs; **GROSS ONLY** = before costs; **CLAIM** = self-reported / vendor / unverified. Dates: research done 2026-10-27; sources preferred 2020–2026.

Important caveat up front: I found **no peer-reviewed or independently audited study of a volume-climax / parabolic-extension fade rule on NSE stocks with Indian costs**. Everything India-specific below is either regulatory fact, aggregate SEBI loss data, or practitioner CLAIM. The academic literature tests cross-sectional intraday reversal (sorting all stocks by past-interval return), not the discretionary "wait for the exhaustion bar" entry.

---

## Q1. Concrete, testable rule-sets practitioners use (run definition, exhaustion trigger, entry, target, stop, time stop, stock/time selection)

### Takeaway
Practitioner rules converge on: (1) a "run" = price stretched ≥2–3 standard deviations from session VWAP (or a multi-% intraday move on a catalyst), (2) "the end" = climax volume bar (~5x session average), a reversal/long-upper-wick candle, and momentum divergence, (3) entry on the reversal bar close or on a failed VWAP reclaim, (4) stop just beyond the exhaustion-bar extreme, (5) scaled targets back toward VWAP, (6) do not fade in the first 30 minutes. None of these sources publish audited win rates; all stats are CLAIM.

### Cited Findings
- **VWAP Deviation Scalp (mean reversion):** entry when price touches ±2SD VWAP band with a reversal candle (doji/hammer/engulfing/shooting star) and *declining* volume on the extension; enter on close of reversal candle; stop beyond the 2SD band by 0.1–0.15%; thesis broken if price hits 3SD; first target ±1SD (1–1.5R), trail to VWAP for 2–3R; **"Do NOT fade moves to 2SD during the first 30 minutes"**, best window ~11:00–14:00 ET; prefer range-bound, no-catalyst days and low-ADR stocks; skip if stock already moved >70% of its ADR; stated win-rate target 60–70% (**CLAIM**, described as a target not a measured result) — [TradeZella VWAP strategy](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/vwap-trading-strategy)
- **VWAP Exhaustion (extended-move fade):** entry at ±3SD from VWAP with a climax volume spike ("often 5x or more the session average"), bearish engulfing at +3SD (bullish hammer at −3SD), RSI divergence or contracting MACD histogram; enter on reversal-candle close only; stop beyond exhaustion-candle extreme + 0.2% buffer; targets 2SD (1.5–2R) then 1SD (3–4R), scale out in thirds, full VWAP reversion 5R+; best on catalyst-driven parabolic moves "up 8%+ intraday on news that is already priced in"; avoid fading earnings/FDA moves where fundamentals changed; stated win-rate target 35–45% (**CLAIM**) — [TradeZella VWAP strategy](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/vwap-trading-strategy)
- **Parabolic Short (day-trade version):** "run" measured from last touch of 20-DMA: small-cap ≥~200%, mid-cap ~100%, large-cap ~50%; requires accelerating structure + an "exhaustion day" (same day as final push or next day; ignore if nothing within 2 days); volume at highest recent levels and first test of round numbers are optional strengtheners; entry triggers in order of earliness: (1) break of upward structure into lower highs/lows, (2) structure break + loss of VWAP, (3) **failed VWAP reclaim = "best entry"**; stop = high of day or most recent lower high; no fixed target—partials then break-even stop; close before end of day; second attempt allowed, then move on; no stats published (placeholder text) — [TradeZella Parabolic Short](https://www.tradezella.com/strategies/parabolic-short-strategy)
- **Qullamaggie's parabolic short:** reward described as ~5–10x risk with higher win rate if you wait for clean setups; no percentage given (**CLAIM**) — [Qullamaggie](https://qullamaggie.com/my-3-timeless-setups-that-have-made-me-tens-of-millions/); a RealTest backtest of this setup back to 2000 shows ~43% drawdown (win rate not shown in snippet; vendor product) (**CLAIM**) — [SetupAlpha](https://setupalpha.com/products/parabolic-short-realtest-qullamaggie-strategy)
- **Volume Climax Reversal indicator (open source, TradingView):** flags bars where volume > N× its moving average AND price makes a new high; optional VWAP filter and wick filter; author advises waiting for a reversal candle and not chasing — [TradingView VCR script](https://my.tradingview.com/script/LCwLc4PZ-Volume-Climax-Reversal-VCR-Catch-Exhaustion-Tops-Bottoms)
- **"Gap and crap"** = a gapped-up stock failing to hold the spike and selling off at the open; definition only, no backtest found — [SteadyTrade ep. 88](https://www.steadytrade.com/ep-88-chart-patterns-the-gap-and-crap/)
- **US practitioner stats (all CLAIM):** trader "Sellers" says parabolic shorts win ~80% for him, overall ~70% — [aitradingkit](https://aitradingkit.com/trader-talks/how-to-short-parabolic-stocks/); GapperLab dashboard shows 58.4% win rate / 2.18 profit factor for small-cap gapper strategies (not clearly tied to parabolic short alone) — [GapperLab](https://gapperlab.com/); a TradingView "Gapper SHORT" script advertises 81.8% win rate but was genetic-algorithm-optimised on only 166 gappers / 70 days (overfit risk) — [TradingView](https://tw.tradingview.com/script/WuHhPb6z-Gapper-SHORT-Signal)
- TradeZella recommends 30–50 own trades as the minimum sample before trusting a setup — [TradeZella Parabolic Short](https://www.tradezella.com/strategies/parabolic-short-strategy)
- **Indian scalper rule sets with numbers:** I found only TradingView listings. "RoboScalp-X" (invite-only, Nifty 50) advertises 76% win rate, 1.61 profit factor, +203% on 2022–2025 (**CLAIM**, paid, no cost model) — [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/script/ZnqLJAW9-RoboScalp-X-Precision-Intraday-Engine); "Indian Scalper 2025" (PSAR+SMA50+RSI+volume) gives rules and a 1:2 R:R target but no measured results — [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/script/KQnZkLDq-Indian-Scalper-2025-PSAR-SMA50-RSI-50-High-Volume-75). Neither is a spike-fade system.

### Inferences
- A testable NSE translation of the consensus rules: universe = Nifty 50 / F&O stocks (no hard circuit, see Q4); "run" = ≥2.5–3 SD above session VWAP or ≥3–4% from open within ≤60 min; "end" = 1-min/3-min bar with volume ≥4–5x session-average bar volume AND upper wick ≥50% of range AND close below prior bar's high; entry = next bar breaks exhaustion-bar low; stop = exhaustion-bar high + ~0.15–0.2%; target = 50% retrace to VWAP or fixed 0.3%; time stop 10–15 min; no entries 09:15–09:45; flat by 15:10 (broker MIS square-off 15:20).
- Practitioner targets (1–3R back to VWAP) are far larger than the 0.1–0.4% target in the brief; a 0.1–0.4% target on a stock that has moved 5%+ is grabbing ~5–10% of the move, which only works if win rate is very high (see cost arithmetic in Q2).

### Gaps
- No Varsity/Z-Connect module on spike-fade or high-beta scalping was found; searches returned only cost pages.
- No Indian practitioner thread (TradingQnA/Reddit/X) with a verifiable trade log for this setup was found.

## Q2. Quantitative evidence, with costs (win rate, avg win/loss, profit factor, net of brokerage/STT/slippage)

### Takeaway
Cross-sectional intraday reversal is large GROSS (tens of bps per day, 100%+ annualised in some papers) but every careful source says costs decide survival; the one Indian review (Subramanya 2026) concludes "transaction costs, rather than signal quality, usually decide whether a scalping rule survives." No COSTED win-rate/profit-factor data exists for an NSE spike-fade rule.

### Cited Findings
- **Brogaard, Han & Kim (SSRN 2024), S&P 500 stocks, 30-min midpoint data Jul-1996–Dec-2022:** buying negative-residual / selling positive-residual stocks from the previous 30-min interval earns **162.3% annualised GROSS ONLY**; authors consider frictions of 0.03–0.07% per trade and restrict to S&P 500 for liquidity (~100 positions) — [SSRN 4731947](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4731947); [CXO summary](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/fundamental-valuation/intraday-stock-returns-from-noise-reversals/)
- **Baltussen, Da & Soebhag, "End-of-Day Reversal" (1993–2019, US):** last-30-min reversal of same-day return = **3.78 bps/day VW (≈9.5%/yr) to 6.86 bps/day EW (≈17.3%/yr), GROSS ONLY**; the authors state the strategy "might not be exploitable by many investors after accounting for transaction costs" but that it is stronger in the extreme deciles (top-20% extreme-return firms: 14.71 bps/day six-factor alpha) and may be exploitable by market makers/prop desks or by timing already-planned trades; reversal during other 30-min intervals of the day is **less than half** the end-of-day effect; mechanism = retail "buy-the-dip" purchases (strongest for intraday losers) plus short sellers covering before close; intraday winners decile "returned close to zero" in the last half hour — [EOD paper PDF](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~zda/EOD.pdf); [EUR news (Quantpedia Awards 2025 2nd place)](https://www.eur.nl/en/news/end-day-reversal-pattern-second-place-quantpedia-awards-2025)
- **Quantpedia "Intraday Reversal in US" (#609):** backtest 2000–2012, indicative return 22.42%/yr, volatility 5.41%, GROSS ONLY; "Intraday Reversal in China" (#608): 2000–2019, 126.34%/yr GROSS ONLY, stronger for stocks with higher retail participation (source: Kang, Lin & Xiong) — [Quantpedia update](https://vvv.quantpedia.com/?p=825); [Quantpedia Miwa page](https://quantpedia.com/short-term-return-reversals-and-intraday-transactions/)
- **de Groot, Huij & Zhou:** short-term reversal abnormal returns "diminish once transaction costs are taken into account," driven by trading in small caps; restricting to large caps substantially cuts costs — [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/SSRN_ID1894614_code340546.pdf?abstractid=1605049&mirid=1)
- **Miwa (QJF):** intraday (open-to-close) returns reverse in the following week, overnight returns do not; reversal stronger in illiquid stocks and volatile markets; attributed to price concessions to liquidity providers absorbing uninformed intraday flow, not news; cites de Groot et al. that daily-rebalanced reversal "hardly generates any economic profit" after costs — [SSRN 3174484](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3174484)
- **arXiv 2605.04004 (MNQ futures, 5-min bars, 947 days 2021–2025, walk-forward):** 14 OHLCV momentum/reversal signal families; **none passed** (OOS t≥2, ≥30 trades/fold, net>0 after a 2.0-point friction floor, direction consistent across 2023–25); 11 families had gross edge of only 0.07–1.50 points, below friction; the "VVG classifier reversal" cleared friction (t=1.26) but was unstable across years — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04004)
- **arXiv 2605.11423 (MNQ):** 77.6% of classifier-positive sessions reversed from intraday peak before close, but only 40 unique days (exploratory) — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.11423)
- **Subramanya S., "Intraday Scalping on the NSE after the 2025-26 Cost and Algorithmic-Trading Regime Changes" (SSRN, posted 1 Oct 2026, independent researcher, not peer-reviewed):** selective review; conclusions: costs not signal quality decide survival; Indian derivatives profits concentrate among "algo entities"; short-horizon backtests fragile to fill assumptions/regime change/overfitting; illustrative calc that the Apr-2026 STT rise "more than doubled the all-in index-point hurdle for a Nifty futures round trip" — [SSRN 7547298](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7547298)
- **India cost arithmetic (futures, illustrative):** a 10-point Nifty futures scalp pays ₹40 brokerage + ~₹366 statutory charges (article internally inconsistent: states breakeven 7.32 pts but its own costs imply ~8.1 pts; elsewhere says 12–13 pts incl. spread/slippage); bid-ask 2–3 pts and 1–2 pts slippage in fast markets — [Marketcalls](https://www.marketcalls.in/learnings/why-scalping-just-10-points-in-nifty-futures-options-isnt-as-easy-as-it-sounds.html)
- **Equity intraday STT = 0.025% on the sell side only; unchanged by Budget 2026** (worked example: 500 sh × ₹102.50 avg × 0.025% = ₹13) — [Zerodha STT page](https://support.zerodha.com/category/account-opening/resident-individual/ri-charges/articles/how-is-the-securities-transaction-tax-stt-calculated)
- **Reversal profits decline over time:** 2000–2009 US sample shows reversal profits shrinking "consistent with the improvement of overall market liquidity and/or price efficiency"; arbitrage capital (hedge-fund AUM) erodes reversal and other anomalies — [SUFE paper](https://econ.sufe.edu.cn/_upload/article/files/d9/42/ca203afb44b38fcefab0073159b6/f6215523-0e6a-4121-a857-52a82a650aea.pdf); part of measured reversal is bid-ask bounce (disappears using midpoints in some tests) — same source; liquidity-provision interpretation (Nagel 2011) — [NY Fed SR513](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr513.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Hurdle for a 0.1–0.4% target on an NSE large-cap (my estimate, partially sourced):** STT 2.5 bps (sourced) + ~2 bps flat brokerage on a ₹2 lakh ticket (₹20×2) + exchange/SEBI/stamp/GST ≈ 1 bp (not verified this session) ≈ **5–6 bps round trip before spread/slippage**; a 1-tick spread on a ₹500 stock is 1 bp, but on a spiking stock the effective spread/slippage is plausibly 3–10 bps. So all-in friction ≈ 8–16 bps against a 10–40 bp target: at a 0.3% target with a 0.3% stop, breakeven win rate ≈ 52–55% before slippage, ~60%+ after. The brief's 0.1% target is essentially uncoverable after friction.
- The academic reversal premium is a diversified cross-sectional portfolio effect of a few bps per stock per half hour; a single-stock discretionary fade needs the extreme-decile version of the effect (EOD paper: ~15 bps/day alpha in extreme quintile) and even that is below a 0.3% target per trade on average, so the edge must come from selection (climax volume/no-news), not from the base rate.

### Gaps
- No COSTED win rate / profit factor / average win-loss for any spike-fade or VWAP-band fade on NSE stocks. QuantInsti/OpenAlgo pages mention NSE VWAP reversion with "honest costs" but I could not retrieve numbers — [OpenAlgo](https://openalgo.in/quant/intraday-mean-reversion-breakout).
- Full text of Subramanya (SSRN 7547298) and Brogaard et al. were blocked (403); cost-adjusted tables unverified.
- NSE exchange transaction charge / stamp duty rates were not fetched this session; the 5–6 bps figure is my estimate.

## Q3. Academic literature on intraday reversal after large short-horizon moves; survival after costs; change post-2015

### Takeaway
Intraday reversal is well documented (Heston-Korajczyk-Sadka 2010; Brogaard-Han-Kim; Baltussen-Da-Soebhag; China/retail papers), is strongest for no-news moves, illiquid/volatile stocks and retail-heavy names, and is concentrated at the open and especially the close. Newer work attributes much of the open/midday periodicity to trading frictions and VWAP/MOC algorithms, and the first-half-hour → last-half-hour relation at the *market* level is momentum, not reversal.

### Cited Findings
- **Heston, Korajczyk & Sadka (JF 2010):** 13 half-hour intervals; short-run reversal consistent with bid-ask bounce plus *continuation* at lags that are multiples of one trading day, lasting ~40 days, strongest in first and last half hours; volume/order-imbalance show the same periodicity but do not explain returns — [paper PDF](https://www.bauer.uh.edu/departments/finance/documents/Heston_Korajczyk_Sadka_paper_UH.pdf)
- **Haendler, Heston, Korajczyk & Sadka (SSRN, Nov 2025) out-of-sample re-test:** trading frictions and trader-type proxies jointly explain **all** of the open and midday periodicity and up to 30% of the close periodicity; open periodicity attributed to VWAP trading, close to market-on-close trading — [SSRN 5749704](https://papers.ssrn.com/ab_id=5749704)
- **Gao, Han, Li & Zhou (JFE 2018; SPY 1993–2013):** first half-hour market return predicts last half-hour return (momentum, not reversal), stronger on volatile, high-volume, recession and macro-news days — [paper PDF](https://c.mql5.com/forextsd/forum/173/intraday_momentum_-_the_first_half-hour_return_predicts_the_last_half-hour_return.pdf)
- **Baltussen, Da & Soebhag:** stock-level reversal in the last 30 min (3.78–6.86 bps/day GROSS) coexists with market-level last-half-hour momentum; mechanism = retail attention-driven buying of extreme intraday movers (strongest for losers) and short-seller de-risking; intraday reversals in other intervals are <½ as strong; the only other significant interval is first-interval return predicting 10:00–10:30 — [EOD PDF](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~zda/EOD.pdf)
- **Savor (JFE 2012):** large price moves with information (analyst-report proxy) are followed by drift; no-information moves reverse; ~80% of large price-shock events had no information disclosure (secondary summary) — [Penn repository](https://repository.upenn.edu/handle/20.500.14332/34495); an intraday jump study likewise finds no-news jumps are followed by significant reversals — [USST lecture abstract](https://bs.usst.edu.cn/Eng/2021/1012/c11213a256155/page.htm); Chan (2003) finds large moves reverse more often when not news-associated (cited via Miwa) — [SSRN 3174484](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3174484)
- **Lu, Wang & Wang (Toronto):** post-shock abnormal returns are asymmetric: continuation after negative shocks, **reversal after positive shocks** — [U of T news](https://www.utoronto.ca/news/beware-sudden-unexplained-jumps-or-drops-stocks-price)
- **China (retail-dominated market, closest analogue to NSE retail flow):** Chu, Gu & Zhou (FRL 2019) find intraday momentum and reversal driven by noise trading — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1544612318307414); Qiu (Nottingham thesis 2022): positive overnight then negative daytime return pattern linked to daytime noise traders trading against high opens — [thesis](https://research.nottingham.edu.cn/files/531719892/Thesis_Jiayan.pdf); "What drives intraday reversal? Illiquidity or liquidity oversupply?" (JEDC 2022) — [IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/dyncon/v136y2022ics0165188922000185.html) (could not confirm authorship/findings)
- **India, intraday horizon:** Tripathi, Dixit & Vipul (FRL 2021), 195 most-active NSE stocks: order imbalance carries private information; short-term return predictability strong for first 5 minutes and fades within 30 minutes — [IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v41y2021ics1544612320316779.html). Khatua & Pradhan (Vikalpa 2014): significant overreaction for NSE extreme gainers and losers, strongest abnormal returns in (−1,0) and (1,2) day windows (daily, not intraday) — [Vikalpa](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1177/0256090920140303). NSE working paper on surveillance measures notes stocks showing a reversal pattern show more intraday volume — [NSE working paper](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/s3fs-public/inline-files/Effectiveness%20of%20additional%20surveillance%20measures_WorkingPaper.pdf)
- **Post-2010/2015 structural change:** HFTs reduce liquidity supply as day-level volatility rises (Brogaard) — [EFA 2011 PDF](http://efa2011.efa-online.org/fisher.osu.edu/blogs/efa2011/files/MM_1_1.pdf); a 2026 preprint argues post-2008 flat-inventory HFT market makers cannot absorb persistent directional flow (trend-following demise context, not reversal) — [arXiv 2607.01550](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.01550); **NSE: algos became a majority of cash-market turnover for the first time in FY25 (data back to FY11); 57% of cash volume in Apr-2025; colocation 38.8–40% of turnover (Feb–Apr 2025); prop desks a 21-year-high 29.8% of cash turnover in 2025 while individual share fell to a decade-low 33.5% and cash ADTV fell 15% YoY** — [Business Standard (Apr 2025)](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/in-a-first-machines-overtake-humans-in-nse-s-cash-market-trades-125041500893_1.html); [Business Standard (Jan 2026)](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/amid-retail-pullback-proprietary-firms-gained-as-trading-softened-in-2025-126012600542_1.html); [BasisPoint](https://www.basispointinsight.com/Story/why-flash-crashes-could-be-india-s-next-market-nightmare_0d82365d77c2.html)
- **Intraday volatility shape, India:** Singh & Gangwar, 1-min Nifty futures 2011–2018: U-shaped intraday volatility; hourly volatility declined over the period — [MPRA](https://ideas.repec.org/p/pra/mprapa/89689.html)

### Inferences
- The literature supports the *direction* of the spike-fade thesis (no-news positive shocks reverse; retail-heavy stocks reverse more) but the documented magnitude is a few bps per interval in diversified portfolios, and the 2025 HKS re-test implies a chunk of "reversal" at the open is a microstructure/VWAP-algo artefact rather than a tradable overreaction.
- Rising algo/colocation share on NSE (majority of cash turnover since FY25) means the liquidity-provision premium that funds reversal is increasingly captured by HFT market makers; a retail MIS scalper is competing for the same bps with ~1–2 orders/sec tooling (Q4).
- Grinblatt–Keloharju style investor-level evidence for India does not exist publicly beyond SEBI aggregates (gap).

### Gaps
- No paper tests a *conditional* intraday reversal (after ≥X% move in ≤Y minutes with climax volume) on NSE minute data.
- Heston-Korajczyk-Sadka/Gao-style half-hour periodicity has not been replicated for NSE in anything I found.
- Grinblatt/Keloharju: not searched specifically; no India analogue found.

## Q4. India-specific: SEBI loss data, broker content, short-squeeze/circuit/MIS-only mechanics, ASM/GSM, Apr-2026 STT, algo rules

### Takeaway
71% of individual intraday cash-segment traders lost money in FY23 (80% among >500 trades/yr), with costs adding 57% on top of losses; SEBI gives no breakdown by strategy style. Equity intraday STT was **not** changed in April 2026 (F&O was). Retail shorting of cash equities is MIS-only (auto square-off ~15:20), SLB is a monthly-tenor product with minimums, F&O stocks have no hard circuit (10% dynamic band that flexes 5% at a time), and ASM/GSM names carry 5% bands, 100% margin and intraday blocks.

### Cited Findings
- **SEBI intraday study (press release 13/2024, 24 Jul 2024; FY19, FY22, FY23; top-10 brokers = ~86% of individual cash clients):** 71% of individual intraday traders made net losses in FY23; **80% loss rate for >500 trades/yr**; 76% loss rate under age 30 (under-30s = 48% of intraday traders, from 18% in FY19); even with 3+ years' experience, 54% lost; intraday trader count up >300% FY19→FY23; **loss-makers' costs = 57% of their trading losses; profit-makers' costs = 19% of profits**; traders with >₹1 cr annual intraday turnover: 76% loss-makers, avg net P/L −₹34,977 (profit-makers avg +₹89,172, loss-makers avg −₹74,575); top 6% of traders = >90% of turnover; **no breakdown by stock type or strategy style** — [TradingQnA summary](https://tradingqna.com/t/key-findings-from-sebis-analysis-of-intraday-trading-by-individuals-in-equity-cash-segment/170633); [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/sebi-study-finds-that-7-out-of-10-individual-intraday-traders-in-equity-cash-segment-made-losses); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/7-in-10-intraday-traders-in-equity-cash-suffered-losses-in-fy23-sebi-study-124072400975_1.html)
- **STT from 1 Apr 2026 (Budget 2026–27):** equity intraday 0.025% sell-side (unchanged); equity delivery 0.1% both sides (unchanged); futures 0.02%→0.05% sell-side; options premium 0.1%→0.15%; exercised options 0.125%→0.15% of intrinsic — [Zerodha STT page](https://support.zerodha.com/category/account-opening/resident-individual/ri-charges/articles/how-is-the-securities-transaction-tax-stt-calculated); [Upstox explainer](https://upstox.com/news/personal-finance/tax/explained-how-the-stt-hike-on-equity-futures-and-options-affects-traders-and-investors/article-189260/)
- **Short-selling mechanics:** Zerodha RMS attempts to square off all MIS positions at **15:20**; CNC sell only if shares are in demat; a short that cannot be covered (e.g., stock locked at **upper circuit, no sellers**) becomes a short delivery on T+1 → exchange auction 14:00–14:45 within ±20% of prior close; seller pays trade-vs-auction difference plus 0.05%/day penalty; if no auction sellers, close-out at the **higher of the highest price from sale date to auction day, or 20% above the auction-day close** — [Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/trending/consequences-of-short-delivery-nse-bse); auction penalty "up to 20% of value" described on TradingQnA — [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/undelivered-short-selling-what-will-happen-now/8036)
- **SLB (overnight shorting) for retail:** exists via NSE Clearing, retail eligible, tenure up to 12 months, only liquid/derivative stocks — [NCFE FAQ](https://old.ncfe.org.in/images/pdfs/Downloads/Others/FAQs%20for%20Securities%20lending%20and%20borrowing%20(SLB)%20scheme.pdf); Zerodha: contracts expire first Tuesday monthly, ₹1 lakh min for lenders, 500-share min for borrowers — [Zerodha SLB FAQ](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/trading-faqs/securities-lending-and-borrowing/articles/what-is-slb-and-how-to-avail). Practical effect: not an intraday instrument; cash-equity shorts are effectively intraday-only.
- **Price bands for F&O stocks:** no fixed circuit; 10% "dummy"/dynamic operating range reset daily off prior close; flexes **5% at a time** in direction of move; since 3 Jun 2024 flex requires ≥50 trades from ≥10 UCCs and ≥3 trading members on each side at ≥9.90% of base (was 25 trades/5 UCCs); bands flex if either current-month futures or underlying hits them; cooling-off 15 min (5 min if in last 30 min) for first two flexes (NSE/FAOP/62241 and later Aug/Nov-2024 circulars) — [Fyers notice](https://fyers.in/notice-board/revised-dynamic-price-bands-and-fo-segment-rules/); [Zerodha explainer](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/business-updates/explainer-on-new-price-band-rules-for-stocks-trading-in-the-fo-segment); [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/newportal/stock-nse-circuit-filters.asp); separate Limit Price Protection check can reject limit orders inside the band — [Rupeezy](https://support.rupeezy.in/support/solutions/articles/21000004845-what-are-circuit-limits-or-price-bands)
- **ASM/GSM/ESM:** ST-ASM 2 stages (≈1 week–1 month, band usually 20%, can be cut); LT-ASM 90 days, weekly review, stage 4 = 5% band (5% also appears earlier); GSM 6 stages, all 5% band, some stages trade once a week/month; ESM for <₹500 cr micro/small caps, 5% band; T2T = full delivery, **no intraday/BTST/STBT**; open positions in surveillance stocks carry 100% margin — [Nitin R substack](https://finallynitin.substack.com/p/the-asm-gsm-esm-of-indian-markets); NSE ASM FAQ lists an Additional Surveillance Deposit of 100% of trade value for the buyer — [NSE ASM FAQ](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/sites/default/files/inline-files/FAQs%20-%20Additional%20Surveillance%20Measure%20(ASM)_1.pdf); blogs conflict on whether ST-ASM still changes bands post-Sept-2024 — [Bajaj](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/additional-surveillance-measure); [Paytm Money](https://www.paytmmoney.com/blog/what-are-asm-and-gsm-stocks/); an open-source tracker of these lists exists — [GitHub](https://github.com/prathmeshgupta/nse-surveillance-framework)
- **Retail algo framework (SEBI circular 4 Feb 2025):** API orders above an orders-per-second threshold are "algo" and need an exchange-issued ID; self-built retail algos above threshold must be registered via broker; brokers must use static IPs/OAuth 2FA; blogs cite a 10 OPS threshold and 1 Apr 2026 compliance date (**unverified against NSE notice**) — [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/sebi-introduces-new-framework-for-safer-algo-trading-for-investors/76294.html); [Liquide blog](https://blog.liquide.life/sebi-algo-trading-regulations-2026/)
- **Broker content:** searches for Zerodha Varsity/Z-Connect or Upstox/Dhan pieces specifically on scalping high-beta stocks or spike fades returned only generic cost explainers (e.g., Nifty breakeven 12–13 pts / Bank Nifty 30–35 pts per lot, 2019 figures) — [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/bank-nifty-break-even-points-for-1-lot/53954); [Marketcalls](https://www.marketcalls.in/learnings/why-scalping-just-10-points-in-nifty-futures-options-isnt-as-easy-as-it-sounds.html)

### Inferences
- Because cash shorts are MIS-only, the "second leg" risk (Q5) has a hard regulatory tail: a stock that flexes its band upward and stays bid can leave a short with no exit until 15:20 forced square-off at a worse price; on non-F&O (hard 5%/10%/20% circuit) stocks an unfillable short converts to short delivery with an auction close-out that can be ≥20% above price. Restrict shorts to F&O-list stocks.
- Parabolic runs frequently coincide with ASM/GSM inclusion (the lists exist to catch exactly these moves); a stock on LT-ASM/T2T cannot be scalped intraday at all. Universe filter must exclude ASM/GSM/ESM/T2T (BE/BZ series) daily.
- The 71%/80% SEBI figures are for all intraday styles; the >500-trades/yr bucket (80% losers, costs = 57% of losses) is the closest proxy for scalpers.

### Gaps
- SEBI publishes no style/strategy breakdown; no "scalper" subset exists.
- NSE ASM FAQ fetch timed out; stage-by-stage band/margin table unverified against the primary PDF.
- Current OPS threshold and live Algo-ID status not confirmed from NSE circulars.

## Q5. What makes it work or fail: time of day, news vs sympathy, liquidity tiers, second-leg risk, why retail scalpers lose

### Takeaway
Evidence favours fading **no-news** positive shocks in **liquid, retail-heavy** names **after the first 30 minutes**, with the strongest documented reversal in the **last 30 minutes** (retail buy-the-dip + short covering). Failure modes: news-driven moves drift (don't fade), the opening half-hour is dominated by VWAP/algo periodicity and retail extrapolation, trending days keep stretching from VWAP, and in India shorts face band-flex/forced-square-off tails. Retail losses are dominated by costs and overtrading, not by lack of pattern knowledge.

### Cited Findings
- **Time of day:** stock-level reversal is concentrated in the last 30 minutes and is >2x any other interval; first-interval return predicts 10:00–10:30 returns; retail are extrapolative at the open and contrarian at the close — [EOD PDF](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~zda/EOD.pdf); open/midday periodicity fully explained by frictions and VWAP trading in the 2025 re-test — [SSRN 5749704](https://papers.ssrn.com/ab_id=5749704); practitioner rule: no fades to 2SD in first 30 min; midday window preferred — [TradeZella](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/vwap-trading-strategy); NSE order-imbalance predictability lasts ~5–30 min — [IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v41y2021ics1544612320316779.html); Nifty futures volatility is U-shaped intraday — [MPRA](https://ideas.repec.org/p/pra/mprapa/89689.html)
- **News vs no-news:** information-driven shocks drift, non-information shocks reverse; ~80% of large shocks have no disclosed news — [Savor](https://repository.upenn.edu/handle/20.500.14332/34495); large moves without news reverse more often (Chan 2003, via Miwa) — [SSRN 3174484](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3174484); practitioner: avoid fading earnings/FDA-type moves where fundamentals changed; fade "news already priced in" — [TradeZella](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/vwap-trading-strategy)
- **Liquidity tier:** reversal stronger in illiquid/volatile stocks (Miwa) but costs kill it there (de Groot et al.: restrict to large caps); Brogaard et al. restricted to S&P 500 for depth — [CXO](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/fundamental-valuation/intraday-stock-returns-from-noise-reversals/); HFTs withdraw liquidity as volatility rises (worse fills exactly when spikes occur) — [Brogaard EFA 2011](http://efa2011.efa-online.org/fisher.osu.edu/blogs/efa2011/files/MM_1_1.pdf)
- **Retail participation:** intraday reversal significantly stronger for stocks with higher retail participation (China) — [Quantpedia #608](https://vvv.quantpedia.com/?p=825); retail attention-driven buying of extreme movers drives the EOD reversal — [EOD PDF](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~zda/EOD.pdf); on NSE, individual share of cash turnover fell to a decade-low 33.5% in 2025 while prop/algo share rose — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/amid-retail-pullback-proprietary-firms-gained-as-trading-softened-in-2025-126012600542_1.html)
- **Second-leg / trend-day risk:** on trending days price keeps stretching from VWAP and every fade loses until the trend ends — [Traders Journal](https://tradersjournal.app/strategies/vwap-mean-reversion); a 3SD print is treated as "thesis broken" for the 2SD fade — [TradeZella](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/vwap-trading-strategy); squeezes are fleeting but unpredictable in duration — [Bankrate](https://www.bankrate.com/investing/what-is-a-short-squeeze); India: upper-circuit shorts cannot cover; auction close-out ≥20% above — [Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/trending/consequences-of-short-delivery-nse-bse)
- **Why retail scalpers lose:** SEBI: costs = 57% of loss-makers' losses; loss-makers trade more; 80% loss rate for >500 trades/yr — [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/key-findings-from-sebis-analysis-of-intraday-trading-by-individuals-in-equity-cash-segment/170633); Barber, Lee, Liu & Odean (Taiwan, RFS 2011): only the previously profitable, most experienced traders predictably earn net of costs — **<3% of day traders**, <10% of volume; working paper version <1% — [Odean PDF](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers/day%20traders/Day%20Trading%20Skill%20110523.pdf); [CXO](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/individual-investing/do-day-traders-make-money/); short-horizon backtests fragile to fill assumptions and overfitting (Subramanya) — [SSRN 7547298](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7547298); most OHLCV momentum/reversal signals have gross edge below the friction floor — [arXiv 2605.04004](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04004)

### Inferences
- Best-supported configuration for NSE: F&O-list, high-retail-participation names (not index heavyweights where algos dominate), no-news spikes, entries after 09:45, and the strongest statistical tailwind for *fading intraday losers long* (buy-the-dip reversal) in the last 30 minutes rather than shorting winners (EOD paper: winners' last-half-hour return ≈ 0, losers rebound). The short-the-spike leg is the weaker half of the evidence.
- "Sympathy moves" (sector peers running without their own news) are, by Savor's logic, no-information shocks and should be the better fade candidates than the lead stock with the news; I found no direct test of this.
- Expect the edge to be regime-dependent: it is a liquidity-provision premium that shrinks as HFT share rises (NSE cash: algo majority since FY25) and widens on high-vol days when HFTs pull back — the same days fills are worst.

### Gaps
- No study separates news-driven vs sympathy intraday runs on NSE.
- No quantified "second-leg" probability (fraction of ≥X% intraday spikes that extend another Y%) for NSE stocks; the only related number is 77.6% of flagged MNQ sessions reversing from peak (40-day sample).
- No evidence on liquidity-tier breakdown (Nifty 50 vs Nifty Next 50 vs mid-cap F&O) for intraday reversal in India.
