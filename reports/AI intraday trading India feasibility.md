# Your 1%/day target beats Medallion fivefold

₹1,000/day on ₹1 lakh is **1% per day, not 0.5%** — roughly **250% a year uncompounded and ~1,127% compounded** ([DayTrading.com](https://daytrading.com/1-percent-a-day)). The best-documented trading record in history, Renaissance's Medallion fund, averaged **~66% gross / ~39% net per year**, i.e. about **0.2% gross per trading day** ([Lenz](https://lenz.io/c/jim-simons-highest-net-of-fees-investing-returns-a1446771)), so the target is five times Medallion's gross on retail infrastructure, and no verified entity in India or elsewhere achieves it. The Indian base rate is brutal: SEBI found **71% of individual intraday equity traders lost money in FY23 (80% among those placing >500 trades a year)**, with loss-makers paying an additional 57% of their losses as trading costs ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/7-in-10-intraday-traders-in-equity-cash-suffered-losses-in-fy23-sebi-study-124072400975_1.html)), and **91–93% of individual F&O traders lose**, with FY25 net losses of ₹1,05,603 crore ([SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/press-releases/sep-2024/updated-sebi-study-reveals-93-of-individual-traders-incurred-losses-in-equity-fando-between-fy22-and-fy24-aggregate-losses-exceed-1-8-lakh-crores-over-three-years_86906.html); [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/retail-f-o-losses-rose-to-over-1-lakh-crore-trader-participation-dropped-20-percent)). "AI" does not change this: the rigorous, cost-aware ML literature shows intraday direction models hovering at the base rate, with only 5 of 40 assets profitable per trade after costs in the one careful multi-asset study ([Frontiers 2026](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/applied-mathematics-and-statistics/articles/10.3389/fams.2026.1888767/full)). The honest version of your goal is a **rule-based intraday system with ML used only as a filter and sizer, targeting 2–5% a month (0.1–0.25%/day) with 15–30% drawdowns**, built on OpenAlgo + the free Upstox V3 API, validated with walk-forward and deflated-Sharpe tests, and treated as a side project funded by salary rather than an income stream. The rest of this report explains why the arithmetic kills 1%/day, where AI genuinely helps, which strategy families have any documented edge at ₹1 lakh, the exact Upstox-compatible stack and compliance steps, and the validation gate you should force your own system through before scaling to ₹2 lakh.

## Costs alone eat 2.2% of capital per day at the leverage you would need

Start with the cost stack, because it decides everything else. On Upstox's posted schedule (brokerage ₹20 per executed order, STT 0.025% on sells, NSE transaction charge 0.00307%, stamp 0.003%, 18% GST on brokerage and exchange fees), a ₹1 lakh round-trip in equity intraday costs **about ₹83 in statutory charges and brokerage (8.3 bps)**, and a ₹5 lakh round-trip under 5x MIS costs **about ₹225 (4.5 bps)** ([Upstox charges list](https://upstox.com/chargeslist/)). Add a conservative 5 bps per side of slippage — realistic only for limit orders in Nifty 50 names mid-session, optimistic at the open or in mid-caps ([Pocketful](https://www.pocketful.in/blog/trading/what-is-impact-cost-in-trading/)) — and the round-trip is ₹183 at 1x and ₹725 at 5x. The table below is the arithmetic that matters.

| Setup | Round-trips/day | Daily friction | Needs for ₹500/day | Needs for ₹1,000/day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ₹1L, no leverage | 1 | ₹183 | 0.68% net move per trade | 1.18% per trade |
| ₹1L, no leverage | 3 | ₹548 | 1.05% cumulative | 1.55% cumulative |
| ₹5L notional (5x MIS) | 1 | ₹725 | 0.245% per trade | 0.345% per trade |
| ₹5L notional (5x MIS) | 3 | ₹2,174 | 0.535% cumulative | 0.635% cumulative |

Leverage lowers cost as a share of notional but raises it as a share of capital: at 5x with three round-trips a day, **friction is ₹2,174/day — 2.2% of your ₹1 lakh, more than double the target itself — and ₹5.4 lakh a year**. A Nifty 50 stock's typical intraday range is 1–1.5%, so netting 0.64% on ₹5 lakh notional every day means capturing a third to half of the whole day's range, consistently, which is near-perfect timing. SEBI's own cost finding confirms this is the mechanism that kills frequent traders: loss-makers' costs equalled 57% of their losses, and the 80% loss rate among >500-trade/year traders is exactly the bucket an automated system lands in ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/7-in-10-intraday-traders-in-equity-cash-suffered-losses-in-fy23-sebi-study-124072400975_1.html)). Note that Upstox has at times offered **₹10/order for API trades**, extended "till March 2026" ([Upstox Community](https://community.upstox.com/t/stay-ahead-of-the-curve-we-re-extending-10-order-api-pricing-till-march-2026/13277)); whether that still applies is unconfirmed, and it halves brokerage but not STT, exchange fees or slippage.

Regulation caps what leverage can do for you. Since SEBI's peak-margin regime completed on 1 September 2021, **intraday equity leverage is capped at 5x and F&O intraday leverage at 1x** of SPAN+Exposure; no broker, Upstox included, can offer more ([Zerodha bulletin](https://zerodha.com/marketintel/bulletin/304576/changes-in-intraday-leverages-from-september-2021); [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/broker/upstox/margin-exposure/33/)). The 1%/day target therefore fails the arbitrage test as well as the cost test. The entities with co-location, the best data and 96–97% algorithmic execution — proprietary desks and FPIs — earned ₹33,000 crore and ₹28,000 crore respectively in FY24 index derivatives, almost exactly matching the ₹61,000 crore individuals lost ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/93-of-individual-traders-suffered-losses-in-fo-in-last-3-years-sebi-study)). A retail bot is the counterparty to those desks. The single most profitable known participant, Jane Street, made ₹4,700 crore in FY25 on multi-thousand-crore capital and, per SEBI's July 2025 interim order, allegedly did it through index manipulation rather than a clean daily edge ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/bs-decoded/why-jane-street-is-barred-from-indian-market-sebi-explainer-125070401063_1.html)). International evidence has the same shape: in Brazil, of 19,646 people who started day-trading, only **3% of those who persisted more than 300 days were profitable**, and the single best earner's daily P&L standard deviation was eight times his mean ([Quantpedia](https://quantpedia.com/retail-day-trading-is-an-uphill-battle/)); in Taiwan "less than 1%" of day traders earned reliable positive abnormal returns net of fees ([IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finmar/v18y2014icp1-24.html)).

Two more things about "consistent" and "small". Daily consistency is statistically unlike any documented trader — even the Brazilian top earner's day-to-day noise dwarfed his average. And nobody genuinely compounding 20% a month on retail capital exists in the public record: SEBI found only **1% of 1+ crore individual F&O traders earned more than ₹1 lakh total profit after costs over FY22–FY24** ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/93-of-individual-traders-suffered-losses-in-fo-in-last-3-years-sebi-study)), Zerodha's 60-day challenge was won by 7–10% of entrants in any window and by under 1% five times ([TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/consistent-profitable-traders-in-zerodha-intraday-what-proportion-of-zerodhas-60-day-challenge-are-successful/14607)), and SEBI's order against finfluencer Asmita Patel showed actual trading profit of **₹12.3 lakh over FY20–Jan 2024 behind claims of 300% returns** ([Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/sebi-cracks-whip-on-finfluencer-asmita-patel-impounds-rs5367-crore-unlawful-gain-earned-from-illegal-investment-advisory-business/76321.html)). Treat any "0.5%/day" straddle or "AI bot" track record without a broker Verified P&L link covering 2020 and mid-2024–2025 as marketing.

## AI finds filters, not alpha, in every rigorous study

The question "can I leverage AI" has a precise answer from the literature: ML that tries to predict intraday direction from price data fails, ML that filters, sizes and times an existing rule helps modestly. The strongest-looking supervised result — LSTM and random forests on open-to-close direction across S&P 500 constituents, 1993–2018, long top-10/short bottom-10 — reports a post-slippage Sharpe of 4.32, but it needed 500 names, 26 years and a 20-stock long-short book, and the authors themselves describe "deterioration" after 2010 as ML spread ([ar5iv 2004.10178](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2004.10178)). Strip away breadth and history and the edge disappears. A 2026 walk-forward study of decision trees, random forests and XGBoost on 1-minute bars for 40 assets found average precision around 45% and, after a single realistic cost assumption, **only 5 of 40 assets with positive return per trade**; the authors conclude "predictive quality did not reliably translate into profit" ([Frontiers 2026](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/applied-mathematics-and-statistics/articles/10.3389/fams.2026.1888767/full)). A single-instrument study on 944 days of 5-minute Nasdaq micro-futures found **out-of-sample accuracy of 50.0–50.9% against a 51.8% base rate, permutation p-values of 0.135 and 0.515**, and unstable feature importances across folds — the signature of fitting noise ([arXiv 2605.17724](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17724)). That is almost exactly your setup: one or a few instruments, a few years of minute bars.

Deep reinforcement learning fares no better. FinRL's ensemble Sharpe of 2.81 came from a test window the authors concede "is a bullish market with an annual return of 32.84%" with an acknowledged "simulation-to-reality gap" ([arXiv 2011.09607](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.09607)), and the only Indian intraday RL paper — DQN variants on Nifty 50 15-minute bars — reports Sharpe 0.74–1.23 on **15 test trades** ([MDPI AI 2025](https://www.mdpi.com/2673-2688/6/8/183)), which is statistically meaningless. LLM sentiment is the most over-sold route. Lopez-Lira and Tang's ChatGPT headline result is real, but the ~90% hit rate is on the non-tradable initial reaction, the tradable drift is concentrated in small stocks and negative news, cumulative return collapses from **350% at 10 bps/trade to 50% at 25 bps**, and the authors report returns declining as LLM adoption rises ([arXiv 2304.07619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07619)). LLMs also leak the future through their training data ([SSRN 4586726](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4586726)) and over-extrapolate trends like retail humans do ([arXiv 2409.11540](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2409.11540)). Indian social-sentiment studies find correlation with Nifty but "no dynamic or causal relationship" ([SAGE](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/23210222241289700)). The Indian academic ML literature on Nifty/BankNifty is almost entirely daily price-level regression with no costs, no walk-forward and no multiple-testing control; nothing in it would pass a basic validation checklist, so you will be generating your own evidence.

Where ML earns its place is narrower and more useful than "predict the next bar". Ernest Chan, after years of running PredictNow, describes pure ML alpha-finding as "unproductive so far" and uses ML to decide **when not to trade** — flagging regimes where a rule is likely to fail ([Algo Advantage](https://algoadvantage.substack.com/p/032-dr-ernest-chan-the-breakthrough)). The largest independent reproduction of López de Prado's meta-labeling (42 instruments, EMA-crossover primary, triple-barrier labels) found a median precision lift of 5–6 points, profit factor improved in 38 of 42 cases, yet **none cleared a deflated Sharpe above 0.95** and the pooled strategy's deflated Sharpe was zero — the verdict is "a precision filter, not an alpha source" ([Daru Finance](https://daru.finance/research-review/lopez-de-prado/labeling-and-cross-validation)). The one controlled retail-scale test on breakouts moved walk-forward Sharpe from 0.61 to 0.76 against a random-skip control of 0.64, with no edge at all on crypto, and found a HAR volatility forecast beat LightGBM for sizing ([kitchenSinkTrading PR#16](https://github.com/shuazs/kitchenSinkTrading/pull/16)). Volatility forecasting itself is the best-evidenced ML use, with hybrids of GARCH and ML beating either alone ([Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10614-024-10694-2)). Rob Carver, who ran systematic strategies at AHL, has a "strong bias against the use of big data and AI in financial markets" and argues simple averaged rules over long windows beat optimised models on short ones ([Top Traders Unplugged](https://www.toptradersunplugged.com/podcast/the-ai-hype-within-investing-a-step-too-far-ft-rob-carver/)). The practical mapping for you: a hand-written rule generates the trade, a classifier decides whether today is a day to run it, a volatility model decides how big, and a meta-label model trims the worst-looking entries. ML cannot rescue a rule with zero edge — the Daru numbers (profit factor 0.75 to 1.03) show it drags a loser to breakeven, not to profit.

## ₹1 lakh leaves two sleeves: 5x MIS equity and long index options

Capital size, not cleverness, dictates which strategy families you can even run. Index option selling — the "9:20 straddle" family that produces the "0.5%/day" marketing — is out. After SEBI's November 2024 framework raised the minimum contract value to ₹15–20 lakh (Nifty lot 65, BankNifty 30, Sensex 20 after the latest revisions), left one weekly expiry per exchange (Nifty on Tuesday, Sensex on Thursday), added a 2% extra ELM on expiry-day shorts and removed calendar-spread benefit on expiry day ([Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/business-updates/sebis-new-rules-for-index-derivatives-heres-whats-changing); [Sahi](https://www.sahi.com/blogs/nifty-lot-size-2026-bank-nifty-sensex)), a naked Nifty straddle needs **₹1.5–2.5 lakh of margin per lot** and practitioners suggest ₹5–10 lakh minimum ([QuintalMind](https://www.quintalmind.com/blog/time-based-straddle-strategy-nifty-options)). Even if margin were available, Nifty at 25,000 × 65 is ₹16.25 lakh notional, so a 1% move is ₹16,250 per lot and the 5.9% election-day fall of 4 June 2024 ([Multibagg](https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/nifty-june-four-crash-2024-cmvmpfycn000s2vpia6oq4kq8)) would have been roughly ₹96,000 on one lot — the whole account. Stock futures and options are similarly out at ₹1–3 lakh margin per lot. That leaves equity intraday with 5x MIS and long index options at ₹5–15k per lot (a ₹150 ATM Nifty weekly ≈ ₹9,750; Sensex with lot 20 gives finer sizing), where a 20% premium stop is about 2% of capital per trade.

Within those sleeves, the documented archetypes and their honest numbers are as follows.

| Family | Best documented result | What the honest numbers say |
|---|---|---|
| Opening-range breakout (ORB), index options buying | Zerodha's Nifty weekly ORB, Jan 2022–Feb 2026, 20% SL, costs + 0.2% slippage | **~48% win rate, ~45% max drawdown**, unrecovered from a mid-2024 drawdown, 2025 "rough" ([In The Money by Zerodha](https://inthemoneybyzerodha.substack.com/p/how-to-trade-opening-range-breakout)) |
| ORB on index spot/futures | BankNifty spot ORB 2015–2023 | 352% total, **48% max DD**, 43% win rate, author flags DD as "very high" ([saimohanreddy.com](https://saimohanreddy.com/orb-backtest-on-banknifty/)); independent replication of the US QQQ ORB paper across 5 index CFDs found net-of-cost returns indistinguishable from zero, 4 of 5 negative ([MQL5](https://www.mql5.com/en/blogs/post/776235)) |
| 9:20 short straddle | Nifty weekly 2019–2024, 25% SL | 65–70% win rate but **average loss 2–3x average win**; 2020 drawdowns of 30–50% of capital; live 15–30% worse than backtest ([QuintalMind](https://www.quintalmind.com/blog/time-based-straddle-strategy-nifty-options)); a 2022 BankNifty 9:45 straddle made ₹29,746 with a ₹23,063 drawdown ([StockMock](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/945-short-straddle-with-sl-25stockmockpdf/256122277)); not fundable at ₹1 lakh anyway |
| Intraday pairs / stat-arb on NSE | 5-min SBIN/BANKBARODA, 2026 | +1.3% gross → **−3.7% after NSE costs** ([GitHub](https://github.com/avadhi3103/Intraday-Pairs-Trading)); PCA stat-arb 2015–2024 +32.9% zero-cost → −32.9% with full costs ([GitHub](https://github.com/kartikya2207/quant-stat-arb)); in-sample Sharpe 1.01 → 0.34 out of sample ([OpenAlgo](https://openalgo.in/stats-arb/the-brutal-reality-check)) |
| VWAP reversion/trend, gap momentum on large caps | No NSE backtest with net numbers found | US VWAP reversion "works in the short run but not the long run" ([QuantifiedStrategies](https://www.quantifiedstrategies.com/vwap-trading-strategy/)); Indian momentum evidence is daily/monthly only |
| Scalping / market-making | None for retail | 1–2% of traders generate 60–70% of F&O turnover ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/markets/stocks/story/zerodhas-nithin-kamath-says-60-70-of-fo-volumes-come-from-just-1-2-traders-526999-2026-04-23)); a ₹2 spread on a ₹150 premium is 1.3% per side and a 2026 market order filled ₹30 worse than the ask ([Navia](https://navia.co.in/blog/slippage-in-trading/)) |

The pattern across every family is the same: positive gross expectancy in a vendor backtest, regime dependence (straddles bleed on trend and gap days; ORB and option buying bleed in chop), and an edge that shrinks to zero or negative the moment full Indian costs and an honest out-of-sample split are applied. This is precisely where the ML roles from the previous section attach: a trend-day classifier (VIX level and change, overnight gap, first-15-minute range versus ATR) to skip chop days for ORB, and a volatility forecast to size positions. The 2022–2026 Zerodha study also found option selling positive in every year with ~6% drawdown but needing ₹3.5 lakh per lot — a reason to keep the selling sleeve on your roadmap for when capital reaches ₹5 lakh plus, not ₹2 lakh.

What this implies for returns is best shown with expectancy arithmetic rather than hope. Modelling a "good" retail system — 55% win rate, 1.2R average winner, 1% of equity risked per trade, 2.5 trades a day, 0.1R round-trip cost (about right for liquid equity, optimistic for options where spread plus charges run ~3.3% of premium) — gives **0.11R per trade, roughly 0.28%/day or 5.8%/month, annualised Sharpe ~2.5**, and in a 5,000-path Monte Carlo a median one-year return of +90% with a median max drawdown of 15% (95th percentile 26%). Doubling risk to 2% per trade roughly doubles return but lifts the probability of a 30%+ drawdown from 1% to 43%. A more realistic post-decay profile of 45%/1.5R yields about +10% a year with a 28% chance of a 30% drawdown. Hitting 1%/day at 1% risk requires 0.5R net expectancy per trade at two trades a day, three to five times what any documented retail system shows. These are the researcher's own calculations from stated backtest profiles under i.i.d. assumptions; real intraday returns cluster, so true drawdowns are larger. Experienced Indian full-time traders on Zerodha's forum converge on **15–20%/year as the base rate, 20%/year with 10–20% max drawdown as "decent", 40–50% with 12% drawdown as "very strong"**, and call 10%/month "not realistic without taking too much risk" ([TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/good-percentage-return-for-full-time-traders/159616)). The achievable band for a well-built system on ₹1–2 lakh is therefore 2–5% a month in good regimes, flat-to-negative quarters in bad ones, and a 1-in-100 base rate of being durably profitable at all.

## Build on OpenAlgo and Upstox V3; buy only options history

The stack question has a clean answer because Upstox's API is free and one open-source project already wraps it. The Upstox API has no monthly fee; you pay only brokerage on orders ([Upstox Community](https://community.upstox.com/t/upstox-api-charges/6552)). Historical Candle V3 serves **1–300-minute, hourly, daily, weekly and monthly bars, with minute and hour data from January 2022 and daily from 2000**, but 1–15-minute requests are limited to a one-month window each, so a full pull is ~57 paged requests per instrument — trivial against the 50/sec, 500/min data rate limits ([Upstox V3 historical](http://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/get-historical-candle-data/); [Upstox rate limits](http://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/rate-limiting/)). V3 also exposes India VIX as `NSE_INDEX|India VIX`, which is your regime-filter input. The live feed is a Protobuf WebSocket (modes `ltpc`, `option_greeks`, `full` with 5-level depth; 2 connections, up to 2,000–5,000 instruments) with a separate portfolio stream for order updates ([Upstox Market Data Feed V3](http://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/get-market-data-feed/)). The official Python SDK is `upstox-python-sdk` (the older `upstox` package is deprecated) ([PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/upstox-python-sdk/2.1.0/)). The gaps you must design around: **no refresh token — the access token dies at 3:30 AM daily and must be regenerated each morning** via OAuth (TOTP-assisted semi-automation is possible) ([Upstox Community](https://community.upstox.com/t/do-upstox-provides-api-with-refresh-token-as-daily-i-need-to-generate-the-access-token-please-guide/13077)); **no bracket or cover orders in V3** (MARKET, LIMIT, SL, SL-M; products I/D/MTF), so stop and target management is client-side ([Upstox Place Order V3](http://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/place-order/)); market orders carry Market Price Protection by default since 1 April 2026 ([Upstox Community](https://community.upstox.com/t/important-new-sebi-exchange-mandates-for-api-trading-effective-1st-april-2026/14822)); and the Upstox sandbox is only a request validator with no virtual funds or positions ([Upstox sandbox docs](http://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/sandbox/)).

OpenAlgo is the piece that saves you the plumbing. It is AGPL-3.0, self-hosted (Python 3.12+, 2 GB RAM), ships a first-class Upstox adapter covering NSE/BSE/NFO/BFO/CDS/MCX, unifies order/position/funds/historical calls across 37 brokers, bridges signals from TradingView, Amibroker, ChartInk and Python, streams quotes and order updates over WebSocket, and — critically — has a **Sandbox/Analyzer mode that paper-trades on live Upstox ticks with ₹1 crore of virtual capital** ([OpenAlgo docs](https://docs.openalgo.in/); [OpenAlgo Upstox page](https://docs.openalgo.in/connect-brokers/brokers/upstox.md)). For research, the `marketcalls/vectorbt-backtesting-skills` project uses OpenAlgo as the data layer for NSE/BSE/NFO and already models intraday fees and a Nifty 50 benchmark ([GitHub](https://github.com/marketcalls/vectorbt-backtesting-skills)). Free daily data for survivorship-aware universe building comes from `jugaad-data`, `pybhav` or `aynse` (NSE bhavcopy) ([PyPI jugaad-data](https://pypi.org/project/jugaad-data)). General frameworks like backtrader, zipline-reloaded, LEAN and Nautilus have no NSE calendar or Upstox module documented, so use them only if you want to own that integration. One caveat: how OpenAlgo's Analyzer models fills and slippage is not documented, so log your own fill-versus-LTP data.

The one thing you may need to buy is expired-option history. Upstox candles are keyed by live instrument keys, and whether V3 serves expired contracts is unconfirmed, so for weekly option backtests spanning 2020 and 2024–25 you need a vendor: TrueData's historical-only plan rose to **₹2,599/month** in July 2024 ([Fyers notice](https://fyers.in/notice-board/revision-in-truedata-subscription-pricing/)), and traders report quotes around ₹4,200+GST per year for BankNifty 1-minute weekly-options CSV from 2016 ([TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/received-quote-for-historical-bank-nifty-1-min-data/135259)). Free Kaggle-style sets exist but have documented strike-coverage gaps ([Kite forum](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/12137/nifty-bank-nifty-futures-options-data)). For equity MIS research, Upstox V3 alone is enough.

Existing "tools" are worth less than they look. Upstox's fee-waived no-code partners are **AlgoTest, Quantiply and 1Cliq at a flat ₹10/order platform fee, locked until 31 March 2026 "with a possible extension"** ([Upstox Community](https://community.upstox.com/t/we-have-worked-with-these-3-algo-platforms-to-get-the-subscription-fees-waived-for-you/13779)); AlgoTest's 7.5 years of options history is the cheapest way to sanity-check a straddle or ORB idea before writing code, but these are options-template builders with live-bot caps, not ML hosts, and the ₹10 platform fee stacks on top of brokerage. Tradetron does not list Upstox on its pricing page and was the subject of SEBI adjudication orders against several brokers in March 2026 ([Tradetron pricing](https://tradetron.tech/pricing); [SEBI order](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/mar-2026/ORDER_1774428858.pdf)); Streak is Zerodha-only ([Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/article/zerodha-streak-review-algo-trading-for-retail-investors/516/)). The open-source LLM-agent projects are reusable scaffolding at best: TradingAgents accepts `RELIANCE.NS` Yahoo tickers ([GitHub](https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents)) and ai-hedge-fund is explicitly "educational and research purposes" with simulated trades only ([SourcePulse](https://www.sourcepulse.org/projects/1797841)); both are daily-bar, US-centric, with no Indian cost model or Upstox execution, and their backtests can leak the future through the model itself ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.29701)). No Indian retail "AI trading" product with an audited track record was found; NSE's 2026 caution notices include an operator running a YouTube channel called "Ai Trading Stock Market Broker" ([Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/nse-warns-investors-against-8-entities-offering-assured-returns-dabba-trading-and-accounthandling-services/81631.html)).

The concrete build, in order: a Linux box or cloud VM with a fixed public IP running OpenAlgo connected to your single Upstox app; a `data/` job that pages Upstox V3 1-minute and 5-minute candles for your universe plus India VIX into Parquet; a vectorbt research notebook with the exact Upstox cost schedule plus measured half-spreads; a rule strategy module (ORB on Nifty/Sensex options or 5x MIS equity) emitting signals; an ML layer consisting of a regime classifier, a HAR/GARCH volatility sizer and optionally a LightGBM meta-label filter; an execution module that submits LIMIT or MPP-protected orders through OpenAlgo, manages stops client-side, and flattens by 3:20 PM; and a morning script that handles the OAuth token.

## Validate with walk-forward, deflated Sharpe and 60 days of paper

The development process is where most retail ML projects fail, and the failure modes are well catalogued. López de Prado's list — overfitting through repeated backtests, leakage in time-series cross-validation, mislabelled targets, ignoring non-stationarity — summarises as taking data, running it through an algorithm, backtesting, and repeating "until a nice-looking backtest shows up" ([GARP](https://www.garp.org/white-paper/the-10-reasons-most-machine-learning-funds-fail)). The tools that counter it are the Deflated Sharpe Ratio, which raises the bar according to how many variants you tried ([SSRN 2460551](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2460551)), and the Probability of Backtest Overfitting via combinatorially purged cross-validation ([SSRN 2326253](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2326253)), both implemented in Python packages such as `purgedcv` ([conda-forge](https://anaconda.org/conda-forge/purgedcv)). Two Indian-specific traps: backtesting on current index constituents materially inflates results ([arXiv 2603.19380](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.19380)), and the Nifty weekly expiry moved from Thursday to Tuesday in September 2025, so any expiry-day logic must be date-aware ([AlgoTest docs](https://docs.algotest.in/Time-Based-Algo-Trading/how-to-backtest/backtest-920-Straddle/)).

The gate your system must pass, synthesised from the literature and the few credible practitioner examples, runs as follows. Data must span at least 2020 (COVID), 2022, June and August 2024 and April 2025, with point-in-time index membership. Costs must use the exact statutory schedule plus ₹20 (or ₹10) per order plus measured half-spreads — one developer's live measurements put near-ATM half-spreads at roughly **0.125 points for Nifty, 0.40 for Sensex and 0.725 for BankNifty**, after discovering his earlier fixed assumptions were 3–7x too high ([GitHub NfBnf PR#164](https://github.com/sarkarrichard-dev/NfBnf/pull/164)) — and Zerodha's own study used 0.2% slippage ([In The Money](https://inthemoneybyzerodha.substack.com/p/how-to-trade-opening-range-breakout)). Backtest hygiene means next-bar fills, every variant logged, and the last 12 months held out and scored exactly once. Walk-forward with roughly 12-month train and 3-month test windows (or CPCV with an embargo at least as long as the holding period) should show positive expectancy in at least 70% of out-of-sample windows and a deflated Sharpe above 0.95 given your trial count. Any ML filter needs a random-skip control with the same skip fraction, and any regime filter must beat a dumb VIX threshold — the breakout study that did this found the ML filter's gain mostly within noise of the control ([kitchenSinkTrading PR#16](https://github.com/shuazs/kitchenSinkTrading/pull/16)). Then paper-trade on OpenAlgo Analyzer for at least 60 trading days on the same code path, comparing fills to assumptions. Then run one lot or the smallest MIS size live for three months, with a hard kill switch at 20% drawdown or twice the backtest max drawdown. Scale only when live expectancy is at least 0.7x the backtest over 100+ trades, moving risk per trade from 0.5% to 1% to a ceiling of 2%, and add instruments (Sensex, more stocks) before adding size, following Carver's diversification-first logic ([Pan Macmillan](https://www.panmacmillan.com/authors/robert-carver/leveraged-trading/9780857197221)). Expect live results 15–30% worse than backtest ([QuintalMind](https://www.quintalmind.com/blog/time-based-straddle-strategy-nifty-options)).

A realistic calendar: two to three months to build data, costs and the rule strategy; one to two months adding and controlling the ML layer; three months of paper; three months at minimum size. You will know whether you have anything around month nine to twelve, having risked perhaps ₹10–20k of live capital, and the honest prior from SEBI's data and the Brazilian persistence curve is that the answer is "no edge after costs" for most attempts — in which case the project still leaves you with a reusable NSE research stack and a verified, personal answer instead of a finfluencer's.

## Compliance is a checklist, tax is a trap

Running your own algorithm on your own Upstox account is legal and needs no registration if you stay within the retail framework. SEBI's circular of 4 February 2025 routes all retail API trading through brokers as principals, mandates exchange-issued algo IDs, static whitelisted IPs, OAuth and 2FA ([SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/feb-2025/safer-participation-of-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading_91614.html)), and after two extensions became binding on all brokers from **1 April 2026** ([SEBI PDF](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/sep-2025/1759232056254.pdf)). NSE's implementation standards set the registration threshold at **10 orders per second per segment per exchange**; below it you are a "client-generated algo" tagged with a generic ID, above it you must register the strategy through your broker with a strategy write-up, RMS write-up and auditor certificate ([Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/general/a-comprehensive-overview-of-nses-circular-on-the-new-retail-algo-trading-framework)). Upstox's implementation: only one active API app per user; a primary (and optional secondary) static IP registered under My Apps, changeable once a week, enforced on place/modify/cancel/multi/GTT endpoints but not on data or read endpoints; automatic tagging with generic algo ID 99999 and systemic rejection above 10 OPS; MPP default on market orders ([Upstox Community guide](https://community.upstox.com/t/a-guide-for-our-api-algo-partners-and-clients/11826); [Upstox Community](https://community.upstox.com/t/important-new-sebi-exchange-mandates-for-api-trading-effective-1st-april-2026/14822)). A laptop on home broadband with a dynamic IP is non-compliant for the order path; a cloud VM with an elastic IP, or a static-IP ISP plan, is. Sharing the algo beyond immediate family makes you an unregistered algo provider, and a black-box provider must be a SEBI-registered Research Analyst ([IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/sebi-retail-investors-algorithmic-trading/)); Upstox classifies anything other than TWAP/VWAP as black box for registration purposes. Buying a third-party "AI signal" and auto-executing it is a grey zone with no explicit SEBI guidance; the seller is almost certainly an unregistered RA, and SEBI censured Eqwires in September 2026 for handling a client's trading account ([Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/sebi-censures-eqwires-research-analyst-for-misleading-claims-handling-client-trading-account/81817.html)). Operationally, Upstox auto-squares MIS equity and F&O at **3:25 PM (3:10 PM for CAS-eligible stocks) for ₹88.5 (₹59 on Plus)**, so flatten by 3:20 ([Upstox Help Center](https://upstox.com/help-center/t-252251)).

Tax is where a side-project trader gets hurt. Intraday equity profit is **speculative business income under s.43(5), taxed at slab rates**, and speculative losses can be set off only against speculative gains, carried forward four years, and only if the return is filed by the due date ([Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/tax-on-speculative-income); [Quicko](https://learn.quicko.com/intraday-equity-trading-tax-treatment)). F&O, including intraday F&O, is non-speculative business income with an eight-year carry-forward and set-off against other business income ([Groww](https://groww.in/blog/how-traders-are-taxed-in-india)) — which means an equity-MIS sleeve that loses and an options sleeve that gains cannot be netted. ITR-3 is required; turnover is the absolute sum of profits and losses, so ₹300 profit plus ₹10,000 loss is ₹10,300 turnover ([Quicko](https://learn.quicko.com/intraday-equity-trading-tax-treatment)); the s.44AB audit threshold is ₹10 crore for fully digital trading ([VJM Global](https://www.vjmglobal.com/blog/income-tax-profit-loss-intraday-share-trading)), though sources conflict on whether a loss or profit below 6% of turnover triggers an audit when total income exceeds the exemption limit, so get a CA's view. Advance tax applies above ₹10,000 of liability in quarterly instalments ([Quicko](https://learn.quicko.com/intraday-equity-trading-tax-treatment)). From FY 2026-27 the Income-tax Act 2025 applies with renumbered sections — speculative-loss set-off is s.113 ([Income Tax Dept](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-113-98)) — and STT on option sales rose to 0.15% of premium from 1 April 2026 ([Upstox charges list](https://upstox.com/chargeslist/)). Combined with your salary, the marginal rate on any trading profit is 30% plus cess, so the ₹20k/month ambition is ₹14k after tax even in the fantasy case, and the real-world 2–5%/month is ₹1,400–3,500 a month on ₹1 lakh after tax — before you count your own hours.

## Conclusion

The useful reframe is that you are not asking "how do I make ₹1,000 a day with AI" but "can I build and prove a positive-expectancy intraday system at all", because the second question is the only one with a non-zero answer, and its answer is uncertain enough that proving it is the project. Every number in the record — SEBI's 1-in-100, the Brazilian 3%, the Frontiers 5-of-40, the pairs and stat-arb repos that flip negative on costs — says the default outcome is a well-engineered system with zero edge, and the engineering that distinguishes the few who survive is selectivity and risk control, not signal cleverness. That is good news for a software engineer: the differentiator is the validation harness (costs, walk-forward, deflated Sharpe, controls, kill switch), which you can build to a standard almost no Indian retail trader meets, and which converts "AI trading" from a belief into a measured, falsifiable claim about your own code within a year and for a few tens of thousands of rupees.

If the harness says yes, the scaling path is capital-gated rather than code-gated: at ₹1–2 lakh you run 5x MIS equity and long index options at 0.5–1% risk per trade; at ₹5 lakh the hedged option-selling sleeve that Zerodha found positive every year since 2022 becomes fundable; and your ML layer's value grows with the number of rules it gates rather than with model complexity. If the harness says no — the likelier outcome — stop at the paper stage, keep the infrastructure, and recognise that the same stack pointed at daily or weekly horizons, where the cost drag is a tenth as large, is where the cross-sectional ML evidence actually lives.
